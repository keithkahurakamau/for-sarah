Dear Sarah,

What I did hurt you.

I've thought a long time about how to say this without dressing it up. I hurt you. I did it through things I said, through things I did, and through the times I wasn't there when you needed me to be.

I made you feel less loved and less safe than you should ever have felt with me. You carried pain that I caused, and you carried it while I was still making excuses.

I'm not going to explain it away. No reason is good enough to make it okay, and you shouldn't have to sit through one.

Full accountability means saying the whole thing plainly, so here it is.

  • It was my choice. Nobody made me act the way I did, and none of it was your fault.
  • Your hurt is real. I won't tell you it was smaller than it felt, or that you should be over it by now.
  • I should have listened sooner. You tried to tell me, and I didn't stop long enough to hear you.
  • Sorry doesn't fix it. Changing does. That's on me to show, and not on you to believe until you see it.
  • No "if," no "but." I'm sorry I hurt you. Full stop.

What I'm changing.

These aren't words to get you back. They're the standard I'm holding myself to, and you're free to measure me against every one of them.

I'll listen to understand

When you tell me something hurts, I'll stop defending myself and actually hear you.

I'll be honest, even when it's hard

No half-truths to avoid an argument. You'll always know where you stand with me.

I'll let my actions speak

Promises are easy. I'll show you, consistently and over time, without asking for credit.

I'll do the work on myself

The patterns that hurt you are mine to fix, and I'm fixing them whether or not you're there to see it.

I'll respect your boundaries

Whatever space or time you need, I'll give it without pressure, guilt, or keeping score.

I'll own it when I slip

I won't be perfect. When I get something wrong, I'll name it straight away instead of hiding it.

Red rose
Love, plainly said

What hasn't changed.

I love you. I'm not saying it to soften this apology, and it doesn't put you in my debt. I just want you to know it, clearly and without conditions.

I love how deeply you care for the people around you. I love your honesty, your laugh, and the way you light up when you talk about the things that matter to you. I love who you are on your hard days too, not only your easy ones.

You deserved patience, honesty and gentleness from me every single day. That is what I owe you, and what I'm working to give.

And if it were ever mine to choose again, I would choose you. Not out of habit, and not because I'm afraid to be without you, but because you are the person I want. I will always choose you.

You have made my life better simply by being in it. I'm sorry I made yours harder.
